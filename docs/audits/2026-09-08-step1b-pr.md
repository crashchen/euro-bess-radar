Five policy replay paths could silently discard 75% of IDA periods when hourly DA was joined with quarter-hour IDA, then return an available strategy result. Reserve ceiling, sequential, sequential reserve, stochastic and stochastic triple replay now require the join to retain both source days before the existing NaN and regular-day checks. Rejected dates remain missing data and never reach the solvers. #86 is merged; this PR now targets main.

The simplified Revenue uplift checks cadence and timestamp phase per local day and adjusts annual uplift by the lower of DA and IDA coverage. Conflicting cadences, duplicates and off-grid timestamps make the estimate unavailable. Days with fewer than two source observations are excluded from statistics and the histogram, while all original source rows remain in the coverage denominators. A window without a verifiable day remains unavailable.

This resolves the review's 0→1 quote reversal: in a five-day hourly fixture, four complete days plus either zero or one IDA quote on the fifth day now give the same 96 usable periods, 80% coverage and EUR 511.35 annual uplift. The single quote previously made the entire window unavailable. Its price cannot enter the shared metric/histogram sample; its source denominator remains 97 rows. The behavior is symmetric for sparse DA.

The ordinary continuous replay shares the same strict join guard, and single-day errors lead with incomplete coverage while retaining DA/IDA/joined counts. Forecast/scenario construction, reserve ordering, SoC, solver objectives and typed failure statuses remain unchanged.
